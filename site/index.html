How it works

One loop, every day. You only do the first step.

  • 01

    Capture

    Write or say anything, from the phone, the laptop or the terminal: what you did, what you have to do, ideas, how the day went. Speech is written down on the device.

  • 02

    Memory

    Your agent files it into your vault: the day, the people, your projects, what you learned and decided. Your corrections become rules, so a mistake fixed once stays fixed.

  • 03

    Plan

    Every commitment becomes a task with its deadline. A morning page plans the day from when you really wake and what really fits, with your routines protected.

  • 04

    Shared

    Every agent you use reads the same memory and plan through the same tools, and logs what it finished back into your projects.

$ myvault capture I have to send the methods draft to Priya by Friday
Captured. It is filed into your notes and plans tonight.
$ myvault morning
# Wed 14 Oct
## The day
- 07:00–08:00 Run, 5 km
- 09:00–11:00 Write the methods section        (due Fri)
- 14:00–15:30 Supervisor meeting
## Waiting for your yes
- Move "Guitar" to 21:00 on weekdays? Skipped 4 of 5 days at 19:00.
$ myvault search sampling
note  projects/Thesis/log.md:12
      - 2026-10-13: Methods: the sampling part needs a diagram.
diary 2026-10-13 11:05
      Took the full two hours. The sampling part needs a diagram.

The apps

Your brain, your day and your diary, on the phone and the laptop.

Brain

Your second brain, in your pocket

The app opens on what your notes know: this morning's page, your goals, this week, and your projects, each with its overview. The laptop publishes them on every sync.

  • The morning page: the day, the facts, the planner's guesses, what waits for your yes, open questions.
  • On Saturday night, a weekly page: what you decided and forgot, projects that stalled, how your routines went.

Diary

A diary you can just say

Log anything, any time. Tap the + in the middle of the bar from any tab, then type it, or tap the mic and say it. Each entry later shows what Vault did with it: the commitments it captured, where it was filed.

  • Speech is written down on the device by an open-source Whisper model. No account, no connection, nothing you say is sent anywhere.
  • Your exact words are kept apart, in a part of the vault only the filing agent reads.
  • Days that are over fold into one line each, ten at a time, and open with a tap.

To-do

A plan you can actually keep

Today's timeline of tasks and habits, with the task you should be on right now on top, and a grid of how much got done each day.

  • A deadline is kept apart from the day a task is planned for; moving one never moves the other.
  • Routines you protect stay on their day. Missed project work goes back to the backlog instead of piling onto tomorrow.
  • No day is planned past 75% of the time you really have, and a plan checker in code says so before anything changes.

Reminders

Reminders you cannot miss, and focus

When a task starts and the phone is locked, the reminder takes the whole screen; one tap on Doing it ticks it.

  • A two-way calendar on Android, and a GNOME top-bar widget on the laptop.
  • A focus timer that knows the task, from the top of any tab, a widget, the top bar or the terminal.

Profile

You, and how it is set up

What your notes say about you, from Me.md, beside everything you set once and then leave alone.

  • Habits with streaks, including ones with steps, such as three meals, each reminding at its own time.
  • Screen time from the phone and the laptop together.
  • Sync with your own server, the calendar, and how speech becomes text.

Agents

Your agents share it

Vault contains no AI and needs no key. It gives the agents you already use, Claude Code or Codex, one memory and one plan, through tools that ship with it.

For example

What did I decide about the second experiment, and what is left on the thesis this week?

On 13 Oct you dropped it: "one done well beats two rushed". This week:

Wed  09:00–11:00  Write the methods section
Thu  14:00–15:30  Supervisor meeting
Fri  due          Methods draft to Priya

The methods section needs the sampling diagram first, from your log on Monday. Want me to plan it in tomorrow morning?

It knows you, and only what it needs

An agent reads your rules, then you, then the index, and opens only the notes the task needs. Your words stay where you wrote them.

Answers worth keeping are kept

A comparison, a plan, a summary of a link you shared: your agent saves it as a note, so the next agent finds it instead of asking again.

Work gets logged where it belongs

Link a code folder to a project and the agent working there records what it finished in that project's log.

  1. Through the dayYou tick tasks and talk to the diary.
  2. After midnightA routine in the cloud reads every new entry, with your laptop off.
  3. It files and plansMemory updated, commitments captured, tomorrow planned and checked.
  4. In the morningYour morning page is on the phone, with what moved and why.

It never deletes a task, never ticks one for you, never moves a deadline silently, and treats a diary entry as a note, not an order.

Your notes

A memory of your own, filed for you

Your vault is a folder of plain Markdown notes in your own git repository. Open it in Obsidian, or any editor. You never sort anything.

What you said

11:05 Took the full two hours. The sampling part needs a diagram. on: Write the methods section

12:20 Idea: record a two-minute summary after every paper, so the literature review writes itself.

13:40 Decided to drop the second experiment. One done well beats two rushed.

Where it went

  • raw/2026-10-13.mdall three, word for word, read only by the filing agent
  • days/2026-10-13.mdthe day in short, linked to where each part went
  • projects/Thesis/log.mdthe methods section needs a diagram
  • Decisions.mddropping the second experiment, and why
  • Ideas.mdthe two-minute summaries
  • people/, learning/, notes/a note per person and topic, and answers worth keeping
  • Me.md, Rules.md, Voice.mdyou and your goals, your corrections, how you write

  • Its rules are in it

    The vault's own README says what goes where. Edit it and the filing follows. Every agent opened in the folder finds it.

  • You are never rewritten

    What you wrote stays as you wrote it. Logs only grow; summaries stay current, with a trace where a claim changed.

  • It learns from you

    Correct an agent once and the rule is written down for every agent after it.

  • It checks itself

    myvault notes lint finds broken links, stale pages and notes nothing points to; the weekly run fixes what it can.

Yours

It is yours

  • Works offline

    Each device holds a full copy. Nothing waits on a server.

  • Your own sync and git

    Devices sync through a free Supabase project you create; your notes live in your repository.

  • No paid AI

    Speech is written down on the device. Your agents run on your own Claude or Codex account.

  • Open source

    MIT licensed. No account with us, because there is no us: read it, change it, run it.

Quick start

Running in five minutes

  1. Install

    On 64-bit Linux, one line installs the myvault command and the desktop app into your home folder.

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Atharv-tw/vault-app/main/scripts/install.sh | sh -s -- --app

    For the phone, download the APK from the latest release. It needs Android 12 or newer.

  2. Answer five short questions

    The installer ends with myvault init. Enter takes the default; --yes takes them all.

    Your notesCreates your vault, with its rules and starter notes.
    About youYour name, goals and hours, so your agents never start from zero.
    Your agentsConnects Claude Code or Codex: the tools and the skills.
    GitHubOptional: a private repository, so a nightly routine can reach your notes.
    SyncOptional: your own Supabase project, for the phone.

  3. Use it

    $ myvault capture Finish the report by Friday
$ myvault today
$ myvault doctor
$ myvault routine

    Or say it to your agent: "what is on today?", "I woke at ten, re-plan my day", "save this as a note".

What it does not do

  • The ready-made programs are for 64-bit Linux and Android 12 or newer. There is no iPhone, Windows or Mac build. The desktop pieces are built and used on GNOME.
  • ChatGPT and other chat apps are not supported: they need a tool server on the internet, and this one runs on your machine.
  • The nightly routine needs some setting up: sync through a Supabase project of your own, your notes in a private GitHub repository, and a Claude subscription that runs scheduled sessions. myvault routine prints the steps.
  • The phone shows what your laptop publishes of the vault (pages, goals, projects, Me.md), not every note; reading them all on a phone needs Obsidian and a git app.
  • Speech models are a one-time download of 100 to 375 MB, and words appear when you pause, not as you speak.
  • I built it for my own days. Expect rough edges, and tell me about them.
